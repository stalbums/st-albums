S&T CUSTOM ALBUMS - HOW TO UPDATE YOUR WEBSITE
==============================================

YOUR FILES vs. CLAUDE'S FILES
-----------------------------
Yours (Claude will never replace these without asking you first):
  - content.js        all text, prices, covers, colors, boxes, album pages
  - images folder     all your photos

Claude's (the design and how pages work):
  - all the .html files, styles.css, site.js

When Claude sends an update, it comes as an "update" zip with only
Claude's files. Copy them into your website folder and choose
"Replace". Your content.js and photos stay as they are.
If content.js ever needs a change, Claude will ask you to send
your current content.js first and add the change to YOUR copy.


ADDING A COVER COLOR PHOTO
--------------------------
1. Name the photo: cover-color.jpg, all small letters, dashes, no spaces.
     Examples: linen-beige.jpg   suede-velvet-navy.jpg   leatherette-black.jpg
2. Put it in the folder:  images > covers
3. Open content.js in Notepad. Find "covers:" and add a line under
   the right cover:
       ["Beige", "images/covers/linen-beige.jpg"],
   (keep the quotes and the comma)
4. Save.

To remove a color, delete its line in content.js.
The first color in the list is the one shown first.


ADDING A SIZE PHOTO
-------------------
1. Name it like:  10x10.jpg   12x12.jpg   8x8.jpg
2. Put it in:  images > sizes
3. In content.js, in the album's sizes line, put the photo in the
   empty quotes:
       ["10 × 10 inch", 0, "images/sizes/10x10.jpg"]
   Leave "" to show the to-scale drawing instead.


ADDING A BOX
------------
1. Put the photo in  images > boxes  (example: 6.jpg)
2. In content.js under "boxes:", copy a box line and change the
   name, text and photo.


ADDING A PAGE TO "OUR WORK"
---------------------------
1. Put the spread in  images > album  (example: 6.jpg)
2. In content.js under "albumPages:", add  "images/album/6.jpg",


CHECK BEFORE UPLOADING
----------------------
Double-click index.html to open the site on your computer.
If a photo says "Photo coming soon", the name in content.js doesn't
match the file name exactly.
If text disappears, a quote or comma was deleted in content.js.


UPLOAD TO NETLIFY
-----------------
app.netlify.com > your site > Deploys > drag the whole website
folder onto the box at the bottom. Wait for "Published".
